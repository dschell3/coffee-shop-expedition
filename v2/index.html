    How to play
    1. The token starts at the bottom-left corner. The coffee shop is at the top-right corner.
    2. Players take turns. Player 1 goes first.
    3. On your turn, move one block North (↑) or East (→). No diagonals and no going back.
    4. Whoever moves the token into the coffee shop wins.
